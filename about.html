About Mentora

A small, independent study-skills site, written for people who are about to be marked.

Mentora exists because of a pattern that anyone who has marked work recognises: the student who has clearly put in the hours and still gets a middling result. Almost always the hours went into the wrong activity, or the answer addressed a question that was not asked.

So this is not a motivation site, and there is no system to buy into. It is two long guides and three tools covering the questions we are asked most: what the remaining assessment has to deliver, how to split the weeks that are left, and how a reference goes.

Why it started

A pattern in the marking

Hard work misdirected is the commonest reason a result disappoints. Technique closes more of that gap than extra hours do.

2026

Mentora published

Two guides and three browser tools, written from published work on retrieval practice, spacing and interleaving rather than from habit.

How we work

Reviewed every term

Each guide is read again before exam season and corrected where the evidence or the conventions have moved on. Our Editorial Policy sets out the process.

Independence

Nothing is sponsored

No course, tutor, app or publisher can pay to appear here, and there are no affiliate links anywhere on the site. Advertising is served by a third party and has no say in what we publish.

Corrections. If something here is wrong, or does not match the conventions your department uses, write to hello@mentora.com. Corrections go to the front of the queue.

What we are not. We are not your institution, and we do not know your marking scheme. Rounding, resit caps, penalties and referencing details vary enough that your handbook and your tutor always outrank anything here. The Disclaimer says this in full.

“Technique is worth more than extra hours, and almost nobody is taught it.”
